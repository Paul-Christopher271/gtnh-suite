📐 Verified GTNH 2.9.0 Nutrition, HungerOverhaul & Spice of Life Formulas
Formula 1: HungerOverhaul & AppleCore Effective Saturation (HungerOverhaul.cfg) In GTNH 2.9.0, HungerOverhaul overrides raw Pam's HarvestCraft values (B:modifyFoodValues=true, B:useHOFoodValues=true): Total Saturation = Effective Hunger (Half-Shanks) × Effective Saturation Modifier × 2.0 Top Verified GTNH 2.9.0 Meals (In-Game AppleCore Values): • Delighted Meal / Sausage in Bread / Beef Wellington / Epic Bacon: 16 Hunger (8 Shanks) × 0.80 SatMod × 2.0 = 25.6 Total Saturation (Raw Pam's was 18 H / 57.6 Sat) • Hearty Breakfast: 15 Hunger (7.5 Shanks) × 0.80 SatMod × 2.0 = 24.0 Total Saturation • Rainbow Curry: 13 Hunger (6.5 Shanks) × 0.70 SatMod × 2.0 = 18.2 Total Saturation • Supreme Pizza: 12 Hunger (6 Shanks) × 0.70 SatMod × 2.0 = 16.8 Total Saturation • Deluxe Cheeseburger: 10 Hunger (5 Shanks) × 0.60 SatMod × 2.0 = 12.0 Total Saturation
Formula 2: Exact Spice of Life Diminishing Returns Formula (SpiceOfLife.cfg) I:food.history.length = 20 S:food.hunger.rounding.mode = floor B:affect.food.hunger.values = true B:affect.food.saturation.modifiers = false Exact Config Formula: Modifier = MAX( IF(count >= 4 && distinct_food_groups_eaten <= 5, 1 - (count - 4)/8, 1), IF((count - 8) < food_hunger_value, 1 / MAX(food_hunger_value, 2), 0) ) • When count < 4 (eaten up to 4 times in your last 20 meals): Modifier = 1.0 (100% Effectiveness!) • For count >= 4: Drops linearly by 12.5% (1/8) per repeat (count=4 -> 100%, count=5 -> 87.5%, count=6 -> 75%...) down to a minimum of 1 half-shank until (count - 8) >= food_hunger_value. • Rotating 5 distinct meals in a Lunch Box means each meal is eaten at most 4 times in 20 meals = 100% Effectiveness!
Formula 3: Spice of Life Carrot Module — Bonus Hearts by Shank-Value (SpiceOfLife.cfg) B:hearts.enable = true I:hearts.milestones.base = 50 I:hearts.milestones.increment = 0 I:hearts.milestones.maximum = -1 I:hearts.milestones.reward = 1 How Bonus Hearts Actually Work in GTNH 2.9.0 (Quest "More Food, More Hearts"): • You earn 1 Point per Half-Shank (1 Hunger) of a food the FIRST time you eat it! • Every 50 Points = +1 Permanent Bonus Heart (+2 Max HP), with 0 increment and no hard cap! • Eating a 16-Hunger feast (Delighted Meal, Beef Wellington, Sausage in Bread) once gives +16 Points (32% of a Heart!). • Eating a 1-Hunger raw crop once gives +1 Point (2% of a Heart). • Total Catalog Potential: 987 Unique Edible Foods = 2,943 Half-Shank Points = +58 Bonus Hearts (136 Max HP)!
Formula 4: Nutrition Mod Nutrient Gain & Thresholds (nutrition.cfg) • 5 Nutrient Groups: Dairy, Fruit, Grain, Protein, Vegetable (Starting Level: 50%, Decay: 0.075 × hunger drained) • Gain per Meal: +0.5% per Half-Shank of Hunger, penalized by -15% per extra nutrient group on the same food (minimum +0.5% per group): Nutrition Gain = MAX(0.5, (Food Hunger × 0.5) × (1 - 0.15 × (NumGroups - 1))) • Effects: - Below 20% in a group: Slowness / Weakness / Mining Fatigue / Hunger penalties - Above 70%–90% across groups: Permanent Resistance, Toughness, and Strength buffs!
